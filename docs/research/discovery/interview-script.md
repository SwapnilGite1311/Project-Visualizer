# Discovery interview script (15 minutes)

## Rules

- Do not describe the product until the last 2 minutes.
- Ask about past events, not opinions.
- Write down exact words.

## Questions

1. What do you build, and which tools (Cursor, Copilot, Claude, ChatGPT, Lovable…) do you use?
2. Tell me about the last time your code broke and you did not understand why.
3. How long did it take to fix? What did you try?
4. When you open a codebase you did not write, what do you do first?
5. Have you ever changed something and broken something else? What happened?
6. Which language(s) do you debug most, and which is the hardest to debug?
7. What tools have you tried for understanding code? Why did you stop using them?
8. If a tool showed your project as a map you can zoom into, explained any part in plain words and showed what breaks before you change it — would you use it weekly? Would you pay? How much?

## After the call

Fill the notes template (`notes-template.md`) within 1 hour.
